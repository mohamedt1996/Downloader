    How this works, and loading a list from your repo
    
    Links are sorted into two lanes. Direct links (any normal file URL, fuckingfast.co/dl/…, or the output_links.txt Moon Downloader writes in links-only mode) are fetched one at a time through a hidden frame. Your browser will ask once to allow multiple downloads; say yes. Host pages (datanodes.to/…, fuckingfast.co/<id>) need a person to clear their check, so they open in new tabs when you press N or Open next batch.
    
    
    A static page can't watch a download once the browser takes it, so "sent" means handed to the browser, not finished. Check your downloads bar for failures and use Copy remaining to requeue.
    
    
    URL options:
